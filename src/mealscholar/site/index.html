How it works

  1. 01Photo That’s all you do. Location only if you switch it on.
  2. 02Foods and how much The AI names each food and estimates how much is there as a low, likely and high amount. No weighing.
  3. 03Nutrients From the campus menu when the food is on it, otherwise USDA. The AI never makes up nutrient numbers.

Nutrient data: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central (CC0). Places © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
